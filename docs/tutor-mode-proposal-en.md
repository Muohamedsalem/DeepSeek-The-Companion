# 🎓 Tutor Mode
## 📜 Executive Summary
"Tutor Mode" is an extension of the "Reading Companion," but in a more interactive way. After the Companion discusses a book or article with you, it can test your understanding, design exercises for you, and track your learning progress. This transforms it from an intellectual partner into a private tutor.

## 🎯 Why This Matters for "The Companion"
The Companion doesn't just discuss with you, it **teaches** you. And this tutor ensures your knowledge grows systematically, not randomly.

## 📊 Core Features
| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Interactive Quizzes** | Tests your understanding after each reading session. |
| **Exercise Design** | Designs custom exercises to deepen your understanding. |
| **Progress Tracking** | Tracks your progress and keeps a record of your achievements. |
| **Absolute Privacy** | All progress is stored locally. |

## 💎 Connection to the Ecosystem
The Tutor is the "builder." It transforms knowledge into ability, and ability into mastery.
