# 🪞 Interactive Mirror

## 📜 Executive Summary
The Companion Mirror tells you about your patterns. What if it discussed them with you? "I notice you tend to do creative work in the evening. Shall we brainstorm tonight?" "You've been asking about a certain topic a lot this week. Is something on your mind?"

## 🎯 Why This Matters
Because self-knowledge comes not from a report, but from dialogue. This Companion dialogues with you about yourself.

## 📊 Core Features
| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Reflective Dialogue** | Discusses your patterns and behaviors. |
| **Personalized Suggestions** | Builds on your patterns to offer opportunities. |
| **Deep Questions** | Asks questions that help you discover yourself. |
| **Absolute Privacy** | All analysis is local. |

## 💎 Connection to the Ecosystem
The Interactive Mirror is the "sage." It lets "The Companion" dialogue with you to discover yourself.
