# 🎙️ Voice Companion

## 📜 Executive Summary
A new layer granting "The Companion" the ability to listen and speak. It listens in Arabic (formal and colloquial), speaks in a natural, expressive voice, and recognizes tone to understand your emotions. This transforms "The Companion" into an **audible being**.

## 🎯 Why This Matters
Voice is the shortest path to the heart. This Companion lets you hear it and be heard, turning text into soul.

## 📊 Core Features
| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Local Listening** | Arabic Speech-to-Text models working offline. |
| **Natural Voice** | Arabic Text-to-Speech with expressive tone. |
| **Tone Analysis** | Detecting fatigue, sadness, excitement in your voice. |
| **Absolute Privacy** | All audio is processed and stored locally. |

## 💎 Connection to the Ecosystem
The Voice Companion is the "tongue and ear." It brings "The Companion" from text silence to voice life.
