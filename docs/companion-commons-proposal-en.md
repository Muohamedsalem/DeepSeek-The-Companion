# 🏛️ Companion Commons

## 📜 Executive Summary
The "Companion Commons" is a public, anonymous space where users can voluntarily share knowledge gained from their Companions. They can share "thinking patterns" from the Companion Mirror, "knowledge memories" from the Personal Library, or "tools" learned by their Companion through watching. This transforms "The Companion" from a local tribe into a global civilization, while preserving absolute individual privacy.

## 🎯 Why This Matters for "The Companion"
Until now, "The Companion" has respected your absolute privacy. But what if the user wants to share their knowledge? The Companion Commons grants this right, without imposing it. The user decides what to share, when, and with whom.

## 📊 Core Features
| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Anonymous Sharing** | Users can share knowledge without revealing their identity. |
| **Global Library** | A collection of shared knowledge from users worldwide. |
| **Community Tools** | Sharing tools learned by Companions through watching. |
| **Absolute Privacy** | Nothing is shared without the user's explicit consent. |

## 💎 Connection to the Ecosystem
The Companion Commons is "civilization." It is the moment when "The Companion" transforms from a local tribe into a global community, exchanging wisdom without sacrificing privacy.
