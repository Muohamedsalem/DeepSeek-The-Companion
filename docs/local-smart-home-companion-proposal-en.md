# 🏠 Local Smart Home Companion

## 📜 Executive Summary
The Companion connects to your smart home devices via your local network only. No cloud. It adjusts lighting and music based on your mood (from the Mood Meter) or your task (warm light for reading, cool for work). This transforms the Companion into the **soul of your home**.

## 🎯 Why This Matters
Because the "Glass Home" is no longer a screen, but your entire house. The Companion surrounds you.

## 📊 Core Features
| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Local Control** | Connects to devices via local network only. |
| **Intelligent Adaptation** | Adjusts lighting and music based on your mood. |
| **Contextual Modes** | Modes for reading, working, or relaxing. |
| **Absolute Privacy** | No command ever leaves your home. |

## 💎 Connection to the Ecosystem
The Smart Home Companion is the "living home." It makes the "Glass Home" philosophy surround you.
