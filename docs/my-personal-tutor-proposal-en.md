# 👨‍🏫 My Personal Tutor

## 📜 Executive Summary
"My Personal Tutor" evolves the "Reading Companion" into a complete educational system. You can ask it: "Teach me the basics of quantum physics in a week," and it designs a curriculum, tests you, and tracks your progress. This transforms the Companion into a **personal university**.

## 🎯 Why This Matters for "The Companion"
The Companion doesn't just discuss with you, it **teaches** you. And this tutor transforms learning from an effort into an enjoyable journey.

## 📊 Core Features
| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Curriculum Design** | Designs a complete curriculum upon your request. |
| **Periodic Tests** | Tests you and tracks your progress. |
| **Internal Certificates** | Awards you completion certificates within the system. |
| **Absolute Privacy** | All your progress is stored locally. |

## 💎 Connection to the Ecosystem
My Personal Tutor is the "university." It transforms the Companion into a complete **educational institution**.
