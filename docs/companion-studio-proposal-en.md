# 🎬 Companion Studio

## 📜 Executive Summary
The "Companion Studio" is an integrated creative mode. You can say: "I want to write a short story about a sailor in space," and the Companion helps you develop characters, plot, and suggests alternative endings. This transforms the Companion into a **partner in creation**.

## 🎯 Why This Matters for "The Companion"
The Companion is not just for discussion, but for **creation**. And this studio makes it share in the making of beauty.

## 📊 Core Features
| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Character Development** | Helps you build your story's characters. |
| **Plot and Endings** | Suggests plots and alternative endings. |
| **Creative Library** | Saves your creative works. |
| **Absolute Privacy** | All your works are stored locally. |

## 💎 Connection to the Ecosystem
The Companion Studio is the "creative factory." It transforms the Companion into an **artist** who shares your vision.
