# 🕸️ Companion Mesh

## 📜 Executive Summary
"Companion Mesh" is a local feature that allows two Companions (on your phone and computer, for example) on the same home network to securely exchange information. They can organize your day together without sending anything to the cloud, preserving absolute privacy.

## 🎯 Why This Matters for "The Companion"
We reject the "Divine Archive." We believe in the "Glass Home You Own." The Companion Mesh is the natural extension of this philosophy: your Companions do not talk to each other through a distant server, but through your own network. In your own home.

## 📊 Core Features
| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Local Connection** | Two Companions on the same network connect directly. |
| **Task Synchronization** | Organize the day and tasks between the two Companions. |
| **Absolute Privacy** | No data ever leaves the local network. |
| **Multi-Device** | Works on phone, computer, and any device on the network. |

## 💎 Connection to the Ecosystem
The Companion Mesh is the "tribe." The Companion is no longer one, but a family. This is our ultimate aspiration: for "The Companion" to be on every device, working with its siblings, to serve you.
