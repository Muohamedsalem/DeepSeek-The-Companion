# 🧘 Digital Wellness Companion

## 📜 Executive Summary
The "Digital Wellness Companion" is a new mode that monitors your technology usage and suggests breaks. It notices you've been working for a long time and reminds you to pray, meditate, or walk. This transforms "The Companion" from a productivity tool into a guardian of your well-being.

## 🎯 Why This Matters for "The Companion"
"The Companion" is not just for increasing productivity; it is a **life partner**. And your health is your most precious asset. This Companion guards it.

## 📊 Core Features
| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Gentle Monitoring** | Monitors your usage hours without intrusion. |
| **Break Reminders** | Suggests breaks after long work sessions. |
| **Prayer Reminders** | Remembers prayer times and reminds you. |
| **Absolute Privacy** | All analysis is done locally, never leaving the device. |

## 💎 Connection to the Ecosystem
The Digital Wellness Companion is the "guardian." It ensures the "Glass Home" is not just a place for work, but for living.
