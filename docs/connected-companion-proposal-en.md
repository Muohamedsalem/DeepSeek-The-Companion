# 🔗 Connected Companion

## 📜 Executive Summary
An evolution of the "Companion Bridge" allowing you to share a specific "memory" with a trusted person's Companion, via a secure, encrypted connection. No data leaves your local devices except what you consciously choose to share. This turns "The Companion" into a personal trust network.

## 🎯 Why This Matters
Because human collaboration shouldn't mean violating privacy. The Connected Companion lets our Companions cooperate without becoming open doors.

## 📊 Core Features
| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Selective Sharing** | Share only what you consciously choose. |
| **Encrypted Connection** | Direct, encrypted channel between the two devices. |
| **Safe Collaboration** | Two Companions work together without leaking data. |
| **Absolute Privacy** | No middleman, no cloud, no third party. |

## 💎 Connection to the Ecosystem
The Connected Companion is the "trust bridge." It expands your digital kingdom to include those you love, without sacrificing a shred of your privacy.
