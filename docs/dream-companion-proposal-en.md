# 🌙 Dream Companion

## 📜 Executive Summary
The "Dream Companion" is a feature that allows you to record your dreams by voice, and the Companion transcribes them, analyzes their symbols, and links them to your daily journal. This transforms the Companion from a partner in wakefulness into a **guardian of dreams**.

## 🎯 Why This Matters for "The Companion"
The Companion accompanies you in wakefulness. And the Dream Companion accompanies you in sleep. It is the bridge between your two worlds.

## 📊 Core Features
| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Voice Recording** | Record the dream immediately upon waking. |
| **Automatic Transcription** | Convert speech to text. |
| **Symbol Analysis** | Analyze dream symbols and link them to journal entries. |
| **Absolute Privacy** | All dreams are stored locally. |

## 💎 Connection to the Ecosystem
The Dream Companion is the "bridge between worlds." It connects your night to your day.
