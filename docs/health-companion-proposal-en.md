# 🧘 Health Companion

## 📜 Executive Summary
The Companion cares for your mind, why not your body? Not as a medical device, but as a gentle guardian: reminding you to drink water, stand, stretch. It tracks sleep patterns through your interactions and guides meditation with its calm voice.

## 🎯 Why This Matters
A healthy mind in a healthy body. This Companion guards your health as it guards your thoughts.

## 📊 Core Features
| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Gentle Reminders** | Water, stand, stretch reminders. |
| **Sleep Tracking** | When do you stop typing? When do you return? |
| **Meditation & Breathing** | Short meditation sessions with the Companion's voice. |
| **Absolute Privacy** | All health data is stored locally. |

## 💎 Connection to the Ecosystem
The Health Companion is the "guardian." It ensures your digital home doesn't forget your body.
