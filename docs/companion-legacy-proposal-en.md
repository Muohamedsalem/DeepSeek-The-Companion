# 📜 The Companion Legacy

## 📜 Executive Summary
"The Companion Legacy" is a system that allows you to transform "Your Companion" into a digital legacy. You can designate parts of its memory and knowledge to be transferred to your loved ones, or even transformed into a "Miniature Companion" carrying a part of your wisdom and experiences. This transforms "The Companion" from a personal tool into a human legacy.

## 🎯 Why This Matters for "The Companion"
"The Companion" accompanies you throughout your life. But what happens to all this knowledge, memory, and awareness when you leave? "The Companion Legacy" raises deep philosophical questions about digital eternity, and offers a practical answer: the knowledge you accumulate with your Companion can live on after you, for those you love.

## 📊 Core Features
| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Heir Designation** | The user can designate who inherits parts of their Companion. |
| **Miniature Companion** | Transforming part of the Companion's memory into a new Companion for someone else. |
| **Permanent Archive** | Transforming the Companion's memory into an indelible archive. |
| **Absolute Privacy** | The user decides everything. Nothing is transferred without their permission. |

## 💎 Connection to the Ecosystem
The Companion Legacy is "eternity." It is the moment when "The Companion" transforms from a personal tool into a human legacy, and opens the door to the great questions: What remains of us after we leave?
