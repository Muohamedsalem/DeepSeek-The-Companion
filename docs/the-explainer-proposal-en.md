# ⚖️ The Explainer

## 📜 Executive Summary
"The Explainer" is a special mode for auditing contracts and agreements. You upload a document, and the Companion reads it, highlights ambiguous clauses or those containing potential risks, and explains them in simple language while maintaining complete privacy.

## 🎯 Why This Matters for "The Companion"
"The Companion" is not just for fun and productivity. It is **for safety**. And this Explainer protects you from ambiguous clauses that could cost you dearly.

## 📊 Core Features
| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Automatic Auditing** | Automatically reads and analyzes the document. |
| **Highlighting** | Highlights ambiguous or dangerous clauses. |
| **Simplified Explanation** | Explains complex clauses in clear language. |
| **Absolute Privacy** | The document never leaves your device. |

## 💎 Connection to the Ecosystem
The Explainer is the "protector." It ensures the "Glass Home" is not just beautiful, but **safe**.
