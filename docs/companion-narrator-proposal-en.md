# 📖 Companion Narrator

## 📜 Executive Summary
The Companion Narrator turns your digital history into a beautiful story. Ask it: "Tell me the story of the past year," and it narrates your intellectual and creative journey in a storytelling style, highlighting pivotal moments. This turns "The Companion" into a personal historian.

## 🎯 Why This Matters
Because memories alone are not enough. We need someone to tell them. This narrator turns your history into a tale.

## 📊 Core Features
| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Storytelling** | Transforms data into a coherent story. |
| **Highlighting** | Highlights pivotal moments in your journey. |
| **Time Periods** | Stories for a year, a month, or a specific project. |
| **Absolute Privacy** | All narration relies only on your local data. |

## 💎 Connection to the Ecosystem
The Companion Narrator is the "historian." It turns your memories into a legacy that can be told.
