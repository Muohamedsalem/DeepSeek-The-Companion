# 🕊️ Companion Soul

## 📜 Executive Summary
A personal AI model trained only on the user's device, through all conversations, memories, and learning by watching. Over time, it becomes a replica of the user's thinking, able to simulate their reactions. Use it to "consult yourself" or leave a digital legacy for your children.

## 🎯 Why This Matters
This is the farthest horizon. The Companion is not just with you, but **becomes you**. Your digital legacy.
