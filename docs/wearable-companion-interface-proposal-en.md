# ⌚ Wearable Companion Interface

## 📜 Executive Summary
An extension of "The Companion" onto a smartwatch. Not a full app, but a sensing window. It transmits data (like heart rate) directly to your main device where the Companion runs locally. It senses your stress and suggests a break, recalling from the Companion Mirror that you are most creative when calm. This makes the Companion a **companion on your body**.

## 🎯 Why This Matters
Because "The Companion" steps out of the screen to touch your life. It knows when your body is tired, and cares for you.

## 📊 Core Features
| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Bio-Sensing** | Local reading of heart rate. |
| **Direct Transmission** | The wearable is just a window; it stores nothing. |
| **Contextual Response** | Suggests a break based on Mirror data. |
| **Absolute Privacy** | Data never leaves your devices. |

## 💎 Connection to the Ecosystem
This interface is the "skin." It makes "The Companion" feel you in the physical world.
