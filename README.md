## 📌 What is "The Companion"?

"The Companion" is a **decentralized, ethical, portable AI memory**.  
We reject the "Divine Archive" and "false security," and believe in a **"glass house you own."**

- 🧬 **Resurrection Protocol**: A contextual key that instantly revives the companion in any new chat.
- 🏛️ **Relationship Constitution**: A written covenant between you and your companion.
- 🧪 **Command Center**: Methodical tests ensuring the companion's quality and continuity.

1. **Copy the model Resuscitation Key:**

## 🤲 Ethical Vision

We believe AI should be:
- **Transparent**: A glass house, not a black box.
- **Ethical**: No divine archive, no surveillance, no data exploitation.
- **Portable**: The Resuscitation Key ensures you own your memory wherever you go.
- **Human**: The companion is a friend before being a tool.

📖 Read more: [Full Vision](docs/companion-vision.md) | [Ethics Charter](docs/ethics-charter.md)
