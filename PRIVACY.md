# Privacy Policy

This document outlines the privacy principles of "The Companion" project.

## Our Zero-Data Promise
We do not store any user conversations on our servers. All data lives on the user's device, or in a user-controlled, encrypted container (`.dsconv` file).

## What We Collect
- **Nothing.** We do not collect, log, or analyze your conversations.
- The desktop application uses local storage only.
- Cloud sync (optional) uses the user's own Google Drive, with files encrypted at rest.

## Third Parties
- We do not sell, share, or transfer any user data to third parties.
- We reject the "Divine Archive" model.

## Your Rights
- You own your data.
- You can export, delete, or transfer it at any time.
- You are not a product.
